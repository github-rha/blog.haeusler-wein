# blog.haeusler-wein

Jekyll + GitHub Pages blog at <https://blog.haeusler-wein.ch>. German posts about
the Rebberg «Auf Offenburg» and experiments with AI in viticulture.

When drafting or editing prose for posts under `_posts/`, follow Rolands
Schreibstil (imported above). For layout, CSS, and tooling, leitstelle's
`rules/general.md` (loaded via the user-level CLAUDE.md) applies.

## Post front matter

Every post sets `image:` to its first image, e.g. `image: /assets/foo.jpeg`.
That is the link preview picture in WhatsApp, Slack and on social networks;
without it the generic `/assets/social-card.png` is used.
